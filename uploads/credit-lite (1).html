An AI-powered solution that transforms how banks assess creditworthiness for college students entering the financial system — giving the 1 in 3 students with no credit score a fair shot at being seen.

The Problem

1 in 3 college students has no credit score — despite responsible financial behaviour.

Traditional credit bureaus only score prior credit use. College students who have never taken a credit card or loan have no score — regardless of how responsibly they pay rent, manage savings, or handle expenses. Around 1 in 3 college students aged 18-24 are credit invisible or unscorable, with young adults making up 40% of America's 49 million credit invisible population.

The result: loan denials, higher interest rates, and exclusion from basic financial products — for students whose financial behaviour is often exemplary.

The Systemic Gap

Credit bureaus only measure prior credit use. Students who have never used credit have no score — regardless of how responsibly they manage their money.

The Real-World Impact

Denied credit lines, higher interest rates, inability to rent apartments or secure essential financial products. Responsible behaviour goes unrecognised.

The Missed Opportunity

Banks that fail to underwrite this cohort forfeit two decades of customer lifetime value to competitors who can see what traditional bureaus cannot.

The Solution

Behavioural Credit Intelligence — bank-grade, API-first.

Credit Lite is a B2B API platform that plugs into existing bank underwriting infrastructure. It ingests alternative behavioural data starting at age 18 and uses proprietary ML models to build credit identities that traditional bureaus miss entirely.
