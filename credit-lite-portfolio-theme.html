An AI-powered solution that transforms how banks assess creditworthiness for college students entering the financial system, giving the 1 in 3 students with no credit score a fair shot at being seen.

The Problem

1 in 3 college students has no credit score, despite responsible financial behaviour.

Credit bureaus only score prior credit use. Students who've never had a card or loan have no score, regardless of how responsibly they manage money. 1 in 3 college students aged 18–24 are credit invisible, making up 40% of America's 49M credit invisible population. The result: loan denials, higher rates, and exclusion from basic financial products.

The Solution

Behavioural Credit Intelligence: bank-grade, API-first.

Credit Lite is a B2B API platform that plugs into existing bank underwriting infrastructure, ingesting behavioural data traditional bureaus miss and turning it into bankable credit identities.

01

Cashflow, rent, digital behaviour, subscriptions, from age 18 onwards. 8+ credit-relevant signals per year where bureaus see nothing.
