The Problem

IHG has limited visibility into who resells its inventory.

IHG engaged our team with a wide-scope fraud brief covering chargebacks, loyalty abuse, and card-not-present fraud across a large, franchise-heavy estate. Through primary research and stakeholder interviews, we identified one clear, immediately actionable gap: 20 to 30% of IHG's bookings arrive through third-party sites and sit entirely outside IHG's fraud detection scope with no signal attached.

⚠ The Bigger Problem

IHG has limited visibility into which reservations arrive via unauthorized booking sites. They scrape IHG.com directly to list IHG properties and take guest payments without any real booking infrastructure.

🔍 The Sub-Problem

Unauthorized booking sites overcharge guests, mask contact details, and set themselves as the booking contact, causing chargebacks, no-shows, and brand damage at the property level with no warning for IHG.

💡 The Strategic Insight

IHG cannot detect what it cannot see. The fix requires two layers: stop unauthorized sites from scraping IHG's inventory, and verify guests who book through them before they arrive at the property.

Why OTA Fraud & Why NAM

North America is the highest-impact starting point.

With 53% of IHG's global room inventory concentrated in the Americas, North America represents both the highest fraud exposure and the highest-impact region for a scalable pilot. A successful NAM deployment creates a replicable blueprint for APAC, EMEA, and LATAM rollout.

Two Scenarios

Two distinct problems. One shared root cause.

IHG doesn't control who resells its inventory. The resulting harm plays out in two distinct ways depending on whether the booking ever reaches IHG's system.

Scenario A

Booking reaches IHG

An unauthorized booking site lists IHG properties and processes a guest reservation. The booking arrives in IHG's system but the guest has been overcharged, given wrong cancellation terms, or had their contact details masked.

  • Brand damage at the property level
  • Guest complaints with no upstream context
  • Booking source untagged: no fraud signal
Scenario B

Booking never reaches IHG

A rogue site scrapes IHG pricing, lists the property, and collects the guest's payment with no real booking infrastructure. The guest arrives at the property with no reservation on record.

  • Guest arrives with no reservation
  • Direct financial fraud against the guest
  • Reputational damage with no recourse

The Solution

IHG Secondary OTA Intelligence Layer

A three-component system designed to operate entirely within IHG's existing reservation infrastructure. No new systems required. Fully compatible with the franchise independence model. Pilot scope: IHG's 8 to 9 corporate-owned properties, with a phased rollout to corporate-managed and franchise estates as ROI is established.

01

OTA Source Tagging in GRS

Addresses Scenario A

Every reservation is classified as Verified, Unverified, or Unknown at the point it enters GRS, cross-referenced against IHG's confirmed partner list. No new infrastructure a configuration within existing GRS that activates all downstream agent capabilities.

GRS layer
02

Automated Guest Outreach Agent

Addresses Scenario A

Triggers on every unverified booking. If email exists, sends pre-arrival outreach at 72hr and 48hr, then an autonomous agent call. If loyalty number only, sends an IHG One Rewards app notification. If no contact data, writes a high-risk flag to GRS for front desk action. Human makes one decision: cancel or keep.

Agent + human
03

Scraping Detection Monitor

Addresses Scenario B

Detects automated scraping of ihg.com via traffic pattern analysis and monitors metasearch (Google Hotels, Trivago, Kayak) for IHG properties on unauthorized domains. IHG already has Akamai CDN activating Bot Manager on top is the intervention.

Distribution control

Key Terms

Quick reference glossary.

For anyone less familiar with the hospitality and payments landscape.

OTA
Online Travel Agency

A third-party site that lists and sells hotel rooms. Examples: Booking.com, Expedia. Unauthorized OTAs do the same without any agreement with IHG.

Forter
IHG's Fraud Detection Platform

Reviews bookings in real time for fraud signals. Only works on IHG's own website cannot cover third-party bookings by design.

GRS
Guest Reservation System

IHG's central booking database (built with Amadeus). Every reservation across all channels lands here the only layer IHG fully controls.

Wholesaler
Bulk Inventory Distributor

Companies like Hotelbeds and WebBeds that buy hotel inventory in bulk and resell it. Unauthorized sites exploit these networks to access IHG rooms without permission.

Chargeback
Disputed Payment Reversal

When a guest disputes a payment with their bank and the hotel absorbs the cost. Hospitality has the highest average chargeback value: $120 with a 3.75x cost multiplier.

CNP Fraud
Card Not Present Fraud

A stolen card used for an online booking where no physical verification happens. Common when payment is processed by the third-party site, not IHG directly.
